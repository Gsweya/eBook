**Measurement convention.** Screenshots are 1179×2676 px (iPhone @3x, 393×892 pt). I viewed them at 881×2000, so **1 viewed px ≈ 0.446 pt**. All sizes below are in pt, and pt ≈ dp for Compose on a ~393dp-wide phone. Treat every number as an estimate (±2pt, hex ±a few steps). The "Apple Books / curated by Mobbin" strip at the bottom of each frame is a watermark from the capture source and is not part of the UI.

## 2. Screens, flows and specs

### 2.1 Reader page (180–182, 190, 191)

**Layout**
- The page background is pure white `#FFFFFF`. Chrome sits directly over the page (no bars or backgrounds).
- Top label is centred at y ≈ 90pt from the top of the screen. When chrome is hidden it shows the book title ("Pride and Prejudice"). With chrome visible it rotates through transient status strings:
  - "Portrait Orientation Lock: On"
  - "Line Guide: On" / "Line Guide: Off"
  - "4 pages left in chapter"
  - "Sample"
  - Style: SF ~15pt semibold, grey `#8E8E93`. In "Key: Value" strings the key is semibold and the value regular.
- Text column: left/right margin ≈ 54pt; the column runs from x≈54 to x≈351pt (~297pt wide).
- Bottom indicator is centred at about 112pt above the screen bottom (y≈795pt).
  - Chrome hidden: page number only ("1").
  - Chrome visible: "1 of 622" (whole book) or "1 of 47" (sample). SF ~15pt regular, `#8E8E93`.

**Typography (book content, default theme)**
- Body: serif (looks like Charter/Iowan-style). Size ~17pt with line height ~19pt (≈1.12 × cap-to-cap; measured baseline spacing ≈ 19pt). Colour `#000000`/`#1C1C1E`. Justified-ish left alignment, first-line indent ≈ 30pt. Paragraph spacing ≈ 15pt. Hyphenation is visible ("Nether-field").
- Chapter heading "Chapter 1": serif bold ~28pt, centred, about 150pt from the top. Under it, an ornamental flourish divider ~105pt wide.

**Chrome buttons**
- **Close:** glass circle, Ø ≈ 48pt, centred at (335pt, 90pt). Fill white at ~85% with a soft shadow (0 4 16 rgba(0,0,0,0.08)). Thin X glyph ~20pt, `#3C3C43`. → Material `Icons.Rounded.Close`.
- **Menu button:** bottom right, centred at (335pt, 795pt). The glyph is two horizontal lines over three dots (Apple's "reader menu" glyph), ~24pt, black. It sits on a nearly invisible glass circle (Ø ≈ 44pt). → nearest Material: `Icons.Rounded.Notes` / `Icons.Rounded.MoreHoriz` (custom vector recommended).
- **Back-to-location chip:** top left, a filled black circle with a white "undo" arrow (Ø ≈ 14pt), followed by a page number ("28", "191") in SF 17pt regular black. It appears after jumping (e.g. from the TOC) so you can return. → `Icons.Rounded.Undo` inside a filled circle.
- **Bookmark badge:** when the current page is bookmarked, a red circle (Ø ≈ 23pt, `#F2453D` ≈ iOS systemRed `#FF3B30`) with a white filled bookmark glyph overlaps the menu button at its top-right corner. → `Icons.Rounded.Bookmark`.

**Interactions implied**
- Tapping the page toggles chrome. With chrome hidden, the status bar is also hidden.
- The top label is a transient toast area: after a toggle it flashes the setting state, then shows pages-left-in-chapter.

### 2.2 Reader menu (183, 192)

This is a vertical stack of capsules anchored to the bottom-right menu button. It replaces a toolbar. The page behind it gets a white-to-grey radial scrim (bottom-right darkest, ~`#D9D9D9` at 40%).

- Capsule width ≈ 272pt, right edge at x≈375pt (≈18pt from the screen edge). Height ≈ 46pt, gap ≈ 6pt, fully rounded (radius = height/2).
- Label: SF 17pt regular, left inset 16pt. Trailing icon ~22pt at right inset 18pt.

| Row | Style | Trailing | Material icon |
|-----|-------|----------|---------------|
| Contents • 0% | **Dark** capsule `#3A3A3C`, white text (always dark; acts as primary/first item) | list glyph | `Icons.Rounded.FormatListBulleted` |
| Bookmarks & Highlights | light glass `#E9E9EB` (~90% white glass), black text | count "1" (17pt) | — |
| Search Book | light | magnifier | `Icons.Rounded.Search` |
| Themes & Settings | light | "AA" glyph (small A + large A) | `Icons.Rounded.TextFields` / `FormatSize` |

- The "Bookmarks & Highlights" row appears only when at least one bookmark or highlight exists (absent in 183, present in 192 after bookmarking).
- **Round-button row** below the capsules: 4 buttons, each ≈ 64×46pt (pill-shaped circles), gap ≈ 5pt, total width matches the capsules. Glyph ~24pt.
  1. Share → `Icons.Rounded.IosShare`
  2. Portrait Orientation Lock → `Icons.Rounded.ScreenLockRotation`. Active = dark `#3A3A3C` fill with white glyph; inactive = light.
  3. Line Guide → custom glyph (stacked lines inside a rounded box); nearest `Icons.Rounded.ViewHeadline` / `FormatAlignJustify`.
  4. Bookmark → `Icons.Rounded.BookmarkBorder`. Active = filled red `#FF3B30` capsule with white filled bookmark (192).
- When the menu is open, the footer reads "1 of 47" (sample book).

### 2.3 Line Guide (focus reading aid) (184–190)

- **Guide band:** a rounded rectangle spanning almost the full width (x≈24 to 369pt, ~345pt wide). Height = one line + ~8pt padding (≈34pt for the 28pt heading line, ≈26pt for body lines). Radius ≈ 10pt. Fill white with a 1pt `#E0E0E0` stroke and a soft drop shadow (0 2 8 rgba(0,0,0,0.10)), so it looks like a raised glass strip. Text inside it keeps full black.
- **Dimming levels** for text outside the band (opacity of the text colour):
  - High: ≈ 20–25% (text ≈ `#CFCFCF`) (185–188)
  - Low or Medium: ≈ 55% (text ≈ `#7A7A7A`) (189)
  - None: 100%
- **Onboarding tooltip (184)**
  - Glass popover ≈ 318pt wide, radius ≈ 22pt, with a small arrow pointing up at the band.
  - Title "Move the Line Guide": SF 17pt semibold black.
  - Body "Tap a line above or below the guide, or long-press to drag it.": SF 15pt regular `#6D6D72`.
  - Dismiss: grey circle Ø 26pt (`#D1D1D6`) with a dark X, top right.
- **Line Guide button:** bottom left, mirroring the menu button at (58pt, 795pt). It stays visible even when the rest of the chrome is hidden (187, 189).
- **Options popover (188)**
  - Anchored bottom left, ≈ 248pt wide, radius ≈ 26pt, glass near-white `#F7F7F7` at ~95%.
  - Header "Background Dimming": 15pt semibold grey `#8E8E93`.
  - Rows (≈ 42pt tall, 17pt regular): High (checkmark at left, 17pt) / Medium / Low / None.
  - Hairline divider `#D1D1D6`, then "Turn Off Line Guide".
- **Interactions:** tap a line above or below to step the band; long-press to drag; the menu toggle shows a "Line Guide: On/Off" toast in the top label.
