# Apple Books iOS (Nov 2025, iOS 26 "Liquid Glass") — Frames 000–045 observation notes

Source: `refs/apple-books/Apple Books ios Nov 2025 N.png`, N = 0..45. Each frame was viewed individually.
The originals are 1179x2676 px (iPhone @3x, so 393 x 892 pt). Readers saw them downscaled to 881x2000.
**Conversion used below: 1 dp ≈ 1 pt ≈ displayed px × 0.446** (≈ original px ÷ 3). Every dimension is an
estimate measured from screenshots, with roughly ±2 dp error. Hex colors are eyeballed and also approximate.
Every frame has a Mobbin watermark bar at the bottom ("Apple Books / curated by Mobbin"). It is not app UI, so ignore it.

## 2. Global design language (applies across frames)

### Typography
- **Display/serif** (Apple uses New York; Android: *Source Serif 4*, *Literata* or *Noto Serif*, Bold/Semibold):
  - Large page title ("Home", "Library", "Fiction & Literature", "Want to Read"): **~34–36 sp Bold**, left aligned at x = 32 dp, baseline ~95 dp from the top of the screen (status bar is ~54 dp).
  - Section header ("More to Explore", "Continue", "Want to Read", "All-Time Bestsellers", "Reading Goals"): **~22 sp Bold**, optionally followed by a gray chevron (`chevron_right`, ~20 dp, #B0B0B5).
  - Sheet/inline titles ("Collections", "General Fiction", "Daily Reading Goal"): **~20 sp Bold**, centered.
  - Big stat numerals ("0:00", "1:34"): **~64 sp Regular serif**.
  - Book title on detail sheet: ~26 sp Bold serif, white.
  - List-row book title (frame 38): ~20 sp Semibold serif.
- **UI/sans** (SF Pro; Android: *Inter* or the system Roboto Flex):
  - Body/list rows: **17 sp Regular** (menu items, collection rows, card text).
  - Secondary/subtitle: 15–17 sp Regular, gray **#8A8A8E**.
  - Tab labels: **~11 sp Medium**.
  - Badges (GET, SAMPLE): ~13 sp Semibold, all caps for SAMPLE with +0.5 sp tracking.
  - Overline "APPLE BOOKS CLASSICS": ~13 sp Semibold, all caps, ~+1.5 sp letter spacing.
  - Button labels: 17 sp Semibold.

**Dynamic cover tint.** The Continue card and the book detail sheet take their background from the cover's dominant color, darkened. For Pride and Prejudice (salmon cover #E9A08A) the Continue card is **#7E4E3E → #8E5B48** (horizontal gradient with a subtle radial highlight) and the detail sheet is **#A4492B at the top → #8A3A22 at the bottom**. GBook should extract a palette (e.g. AndroidX Palette `darkVibrant`/`darkMuted`) and derive these.

### Liquid Glass components
- **Glass circle button** (back, close, •••, sort, search when minimized): diameter **44 dp**. Fill is white at ~70–85% plus a blur of what is behind it (~20 dp), with a 0.5 dp white inner rim highlight and a very soft shadow (y 4 dp, blur 16 dp, black 8%). Icon ~22 dp, black.
  Over colored content the glass **takes on the tint underneath** (frame 14: green-tinted home circle over a green cover, pink search circle over a pink cover; frame 20: peach circles on the rust sheet).
- **Glass pill group** (frame 20/25 "✓  •••"): ~92 x 40 dp, full radius, two icons spaced ~46 dp apart.
- **Edit pill** (frame 35): ~54 x 44 dp, text "Edit" 17 sp.
- **Floating tab bar**: a pill at x 21→302 dp, **~281 x 62 dp**, full radius, ~21 dp above the bottom of the screen (just above the home indicator). It holds 3 items: Home (`home` filled), Library (shelf-of-books icon; Material `shelves` / `library_books`), Book Store (`shopping_bag` filled). Each item is ~94 dp wide with the icon (~26 dp) above the label (11 sp).
  The **selected item** sits on an inner pill (lens) about 95 x 54 dp, fill #EDEDED at ~70% on light content. When the bar is over colored covers, the whole bar and lens tint warm orange/yellow (frame 3).
  A **separate search circle** of **62 dp** (`search`) sits 8 dp to the right of the bar.
- **Tab bar minimize on scroll** (frames 10, 11, 13, 14, 21, 24, 25): scrolling down collapses the bar into a single **~47 dp** glass circle on the left showing only the current tab's icon. The search circle shrinks to the same ~47 dp on the right. Scrolling up or reaching the end expands it again (frames 12, 16). Animate it as a morph (shrink width, fade the labels).
- **Scroll-edge effect**: there is no opaque top bar. Content scrolls under the status bar, and the pinned glass controls (ring, avatar, back) float over it. A soft white fade or blur of ~60–70 dp sits at the top (frame 16) and at the bottom behind the tab bar (frame 24).
- **Glass menu / popover** (18, 19, 23, 33): ~248 dp wide, corner radius **~27 dp**, frosted white (~80%) with a strong blur that shows colors bleeding through (blue cover visible in 18). Rows are ~42 dp tall, with a 24 dp outline icon at x+26 dp and text at x+60 dp. Group dividers are inset hairlines. It grows out of the ••• button that triggered it.
- **Glass alert** (2, 44): ~319 dp wide, radius **~28–32 dp**, padding 30 dp. Title 17 sp Semibold left aligned, message 15–17 sp gray left aligned. Two side-by-side pill buttons ~140 x 47 dp in translucent gray (#E3E3E5 at ~70%). Destructive label is red. Scrim is black at ~25%.

### Book covers
- Covers have a **3D book look**: a darker spine strip ~3–4 dp on the left edge with a light highlight line, a tiny corner radius (~2 dp), and a soft drop shadow (y ~8 dp, blur ~20 dp, black ~18%).
- Covers keep their native aspect ratio and are **bottom-aligned** in carousels and grids, so their tops differ (frames 14, 17, 32).
- Carousel cover width **~156 dp** (height ~244 dp at 2:3.1), gap **~16 dp**, left inset 32 dp. The next cover peeks at the right edge. Scrolling is horizontal with snapping.

## 3. Screens / flows with specs

### A. Onboarding (frames 0–2)
- **Container**: a modal card filling the screen below the status bar, top corners radius **~36 dp**, white on a #CFCFCF backdrop (looks like a pushed-back sheet).
- **Welcome (0)**: app icon 82 dp, radius ~18 dp (squircle), centered at y ≈ 175 dp. Title "Welcome to Apple Books" 28 sp Bold sans, left aligned at x 38 dp. Subtitle "Find great books and audiobooks you can read or listen to anywhere, on all your Apple devices." 28 sp Regular in #8A8A8E. "Features vary by region." 13 sp gray. Privacy block near the bottom: a two-people icon (Material `handshake` / `people`) 30 dp, then 13 sp gray text ending in an underlined link "See how your data is managed and how to change your preferences…".
  Button "Get Started": 316 x 50 dp, full radius, #1C1C1E fill, white 17 sp Semibold, bottom margin ~40 dp.
- **Discover New Books (1)**: the illustration takes the top ~45% (a lock-screen mock with a notification card radius ~20 dp, flashlight and camera circles). Title centered 28 sp Bold "Discover New Books". Body centered 22 sp gray "Get notified about new books you might like, and when you’ve reached your daily reading goal."
  "Continue" pill 316 x 50 dp in #3A3A3C. "Not Now" is a 17 sp Medium black text button 40 dp below it.
- **Permission (2)**: the system alert (see Glass alert). On Android, map it to the `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` runtime prompt, shown only after the user taps "Continue".
