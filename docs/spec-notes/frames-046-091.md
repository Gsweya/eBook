### Glass surfaces
- **Glass circle button:** 44 dp diameter (98 px). Fill is white at about 85–92% over blur. Shadow is soft, roughly `0 4 16 rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`. Icon is 20–22 dp, black, regular stroke. Positioned 16 dp from the screen edge, top edge about 59 dp from the top (just under the status bar).
- **Glass pill button** ("Edit", "Select All", "Sections"): 44 dp tall, fully rounded, 18–20 dp horizontal padding, 17 sp regular black label. Widths seen: Edit 54 dp, Select All 97 dp, Sections 90 dp.
- **Two-segment capsule** on the product page ("+" and "•••"): 91 x 40–44 dp, one capsule containing two icons with no visible divider.
- **Tinted glass:** on coloured product and series headers, the glass buttons take the theme colour. They become a lighter translucent tint of it (for example `#F3B9A3` at about 80% on rust), and on the series page they get a darker brown fill with a 1.5 dp orange-brown rim (`#C2602E`). Over scrolling covers, the tab bar and buttons visibly pick up the colours behind them (pink, green, orange in 65, 72, 86, 89). On Android, approximate this with a translucent fill plus a blur (RenderEffect on API 31+) and a 1 dp light inner rim.
- **Glass popover menu** (47, 53, 55, 71): fill white at about 80% over blur, about 28 dp corner radius, width about 248 dp. It anchors at the top-right button and grows out of it (the ••• button morphs into the menu). Rows are 48–56 dp tall with 17 sp labels, a 22 dp leading icon at x+28 dp and the label at x+60 dp. A ✓ checkmark sits in a leading column (x+14 dp) for selected options. Section header ("Sort by…", "Book Store Sections") is 15 sp in `#8E8E93`. Groups are split by full-width hairlines with 24 dp horizontal insets. A submenu row ends in a ">" chevron. When opened, the submenu **expands inline as a raised nested card** (53): its header turns bold with a "⌄" chevron, and the parent's other rows dim to about 40%.

## 3. Screens and flows: specs

### 3.1 Floating tab bar (Liquid Glass) — 47, 52–55, 59, 61–73
- **Expanded:** a glass capsule from x 21 to 302 dp (281 x 61 dp), bottom about 22 dp above the home indicator, holding 3 tabs: Home, Library, Book Store. **Search is a separate 62 dp glass circle** to the right (x 310–372 dp), vertically centred with the capsule.
- **Tab item:** 28 dp icon over an 11–12 sp label, filled black. **The selected tab sits on an inner pill** about 95 x 52 dp, filled `#EDEDED` (light grey, glass-like), fully rounded, inset about 4 dp in the capsule.
- **Collapse on scroll (67–69):** scrolling down shrinks the capsule into a **single 48 dp circle showing only the active tab's icon** (bottom-left at x 28 dp). Search shrinks to a 48 dp circle at bottom-right (x 318 dp). Labels disappear. **Re-expands** on scroll up or at the end of the content (70). Animate with a shared-element morph, about 300 ms spring.
- **Search mode (74–90):** when Search is active, the bar becomes: [Home 48 dp circle] + [search field pill 277 x 47 dp, placeholder "Apple Books", leading magnifier `#8E8E93`, 17 sp] and, while there is a query or a modal, + [X circle 48 dp]. The Home circle shows the last non-search tab icon and tapping it exits search.
- Glass tint: the bar refracts content colours behind it (pink, green, orange casts are visible).
