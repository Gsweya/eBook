# Apple Books iOS (Nov 2025, iOS 26 "Liquid Glass"): frames 092-133

Spec notes for the GBook Android clone (Kotlin/Compose).

**Measurement basis.** Source frames are 1179 x 2676 px: an iPhone at 393 pt wide, plus a Mobbin footer about 120 px tall. All sizes below are in **pt ≈ dp**, converted from what was measured (1 pt = 3 source px = 2.24 displayed px). Expect ±2 dp on sizes. Hex colors are estimates read off the screenshots, not sampled values. "Serif" means Apple's New York-style display serif; on Android, use a bundled serif such as Source Serif 4 or Literata for UI headings. "SF" means the system sans; use Inter or Roboto Flex.

## 2. Global Liquid Glass patterns seen in these frames

### 2.1 Floating glass circle buttons (nav)
- **Size:** 44 dp circle. Center at x = 38 dp from the left (or right) edge, y = 81 dp from the top of the screen. That leaves about 16 dp side inset and puts the button in the nav row just below the status bar (status bar text baseline at about y 29 dp).
- **Fill on white content:** white, about 70% alpha, with backdrop blur (about 20 dp radius). Soft shadow: y 4, blur 16, `#000000` at 8-10%. Hairline highlight stroke on the top edge, white at 60%.
- **Fill on coloured content (brown hero, 094-097 / rust page, 116):** the glass takes on the content color. The brown page gives about `#8A4520` at 70%; the rust page gives a peach-tinted glass `#F2A88C` at about 85% with a darker glyph `#5A2414`. The edge picks up a lighter rim (`#C8703F`).
- **Pressed:** the fill darkens (096: ⋯ button becomes `#4A2010` on brown). Use a scale of about 0.96 too.
- **Glyph:** 20-22 dp, regular or medium weight, `#1C1C1E` on light glass and `#FFFFFF`/peach on dark.
- **Grouped capsule (116):** [+ │ ⋯] in one capsule, 99 x 44 dp, two 20 dp glyphs, with no visible divider. It's a merged glass group.
- **Icons:** back = `Icons.AutoMirrored.Rounded.ArrowBackIos` (or `ChevronLeft`); more = `Icons.Rounded.MoreHoriz`; close = `Icons.Rounded.Close`; add = `Icons.Rounded.Add`; compose = `Icons.Outlined.RateReview` / Material Symbols `edit_square`; share = `Icons.Rounded.IosShare`.

### 2.2 Bottom accessory bar with search active (092-104, 116, 130-131)
- There are three floating glass elements and no full-width bar. Their bottom edge sits about 26 dp above the screen's bottom edge; part of that area is hidden by the Mobbin footer, so treat it as about 20-28 dp.
  1. **Collapsed tab circle**, 47 dp, at the left (x center about 52 dp). It shows the current tab's filled glyph: **home** (`Icons.Rounded.Home`) on the Home stack, **shopping bag** (`Icons.Rounded.ShoppingBag`) on the Store stack (101-104). This is the iOS 26 behaviour where the tab bar collapses to one bubble while search is active.
  2. **Search pill**, about 216 x 47 dp, fully rounded. Contains a magnifier glyph (20 dp, `#3C3C43`) at 16 dp padding, query text "Pride" at 20 sp regular `#000`, and a clear button (`Icons.Rounded.Cancel`, 18 dp, `#8E8E93`) at the right.
  3. **Close-search circle**, 47 dp, with an X glyph of 24 dp.
- **Gaps** between the elements are about 12 dp. Fill is white at about 75% with blur and a shadow (y 6, blur 20, `#000` at 10%).
- Content scrolls under the bar. The bottom edge uses a progressive blur/fade (white to transparent over about 40 dp).

### 2.3 Top scroll edge
- Content passes under the status bar and nav buttons with a **progressive blur plus white fade** (099, 102). There is no hard bar or hairline.
- Nav titles are centered between the circles, vertically aligned with the circle centers (y about 81 dp). They use the serif at about 22 sp bold.

### 2.4 Pull-down menus (094 dark, 103 light)
- **Width** about 248 dp, **corner radius** about 24 dp. The menu anchors to the source button: it grows from the ⋯ button at top right in 094, and from "Sort" at the right in 103.
- **Rows:** 42 dp tall. Label 20 sp regular (`#FFFFFF` on dark, `#000` on light). A leading checkmark column about 28 dp wide holds `Icons.Rounded.Check` at 18 dp; label text starts at about 40 dp.
- **Section headers** ("View", "Sort by"): 15 sp regular, secondary (white at 55% on dark, `#8E8E93` on light), with a 1 px separator above each section (white at 12% on dark, `#E5E5EA` on light).
- **Leading-icon row** ("Share"): `IosShare` glyph 22 dp, then the label.
- **Dark variant fill:** `#3A1C0C` at about 88% plus blur, tinted by the page. **Light variant:** `#F7F7F8` at about 85% plus blur, shadow y 10 / blur 30 / 15%.
- **Pressed row** shows the label in the accent color (094 "Purchases" turns orange `#F0883A`).
- **Interactions:** tap selects one item per section, and the menu closes. The list reloads with skeletons (096), then shows results (097).

### 2.5 Alerts (109, 110, 112)
- **Width** about 318 dp, **corner radius** about 30 dp. Fill is light glass `#F2F2F4` at about 92%, with a white rim stroke and the background dimmed by `#000` at 20%.
- **Padding:** about 30 dp horizontal and about 24 dp top.
- **Title:** 20 sp semibold, left-aligned (not centered as in older iOS). **Message:** 17 sp regular `#8E8E93`, left-aligned.
- **Text field:** pill 47 dp tall, fill `#E3E3E8`, placeholder `#B0B0B5` 20 sp, 16 dp inner padding.
- **Buttons:** pills 47 dp tall, fill `#E3E3E8`, label 20 sp regular `#000`, 8 dp gap. Two side by side (each about 140 dp), or one full width (112).
- **Disabled** label is `#B8B8BD` (the "OK" button before any text is entered).

### 2.6 Anchored confirmation popover (115)
- Glass bubble about 239 dp wide with a corner radius of about 27 dp. It is centered on, and overlaps, the button that triggered it.
- Message: 20 sp regular `#000`, left-aligned, about 30 dp padding.
- Destructive pill: full width, 47 dp tall, fill `#E8E8EC`, label `#E5484D` at 20 sp.
- The rest of the screen gets a light white veil.

### 2.7 Sheets
- **Page sheet (105-113, 117):** top edge at y about 59 dp. The parent stays visible behind it, shrunk and dimmed to grey `#C8C8C8`. Top corner radius about 34 dp, sheet background `#FFFFFF`.
- **Stacked sheet (118):** a second sheet over the first. The first shows as a narrower card peeking about 10 dp above, inset about 16 dp on each side, with a grey `#E0E0E0` top edge.
- **Floating sheet (128-129):** inset about 6 dp from the sides and bottom, all corners about 40 dp (concentric with the device). The header is tinted by the underlying page color (rose `#E6C3BC`). Inside is a white card with corner radius about 20 dp. A home indicator bar appears inside the sheet at the bottom (129).
- **Sheet header buttons:** X circle (44 dp) at top left, ✓ circle (44 dp) at top right. ✓ enabled is fill `#3A3A3C` with a white glyph. ✓ disabled is fill `#8E8E93` at 70% with a white glyph at 80% (105, 115).
- **Interactions:** swipe down to dismiss (111 shows the sheet sliding down).

### 2.8 Skeleton loading (096)
- Cover placeholder: 62 x 93 dp rect `#D9D9D9`, square corners. Text bars about 9 dp tall `#DADADA`, corner radius 2 dp, widths roughly 200 / 113 / 69 dp.
- GET placeholder: a pill `#F0F0F3` with an inner bar `#D0D0D5` (about 21 x 10 dp).
- No shimmer is visible in the still frame. A subtle pulse is optional.
